Probashi Ranna brings Bangladeshi home cooking to Bengalis living abroad: step-by-step recipes in Bengali or English, and for every ingredient the name it goes by in the shops where you live. No account, no ads.

Coming soon on Google Play and the App Store

What Probashi Ranna does

Ingredient names for your local shops

দোকানে কী নামে পাবেন

Pick the country you live in and every ingredient also shows the name used in shops there. You choose the country yourself; the app never asks for your location.

Adjust the serving size

পরিমাণ বদলান

Change the number of servings and the ingredient amounts adjust with it.

One shopping list

বাজারের তালিকা

Add a recipe's ingredients to your list. The same ingredient from several recipes is merged into one amount, and you can share the list.

Cook mode with step timers

রান্নার সময় ধাপে ধাপে

Follow the recipe one step at a time and start a timer right at the step that needs one.

Favorites and search

পছন্দ আর খোঁজা

Search the recipes and keep the ones you cook most in your favorites.

বাংলা or English, light or dark

আপনার পছন্দের ভাষায়

Use the app in Bengali or English, with a light or dark look.

Questions

Do I need an account?

No. On first start the app registers an anonymous installation with our server – without your name, e-mail or phone number. Details in the privacy policy.

Does the app know where I live?

Only the country you pick yourself. The app doesn't use your location.

Do I need the internet?

For the free version, yes: recipes load online. With Premium you download the offline pack for your country and can then cook without a connection.

Is Premium a subscription?

No. Premium is a one-time purchase through Google Play or the App Store. The price is shown in the store before you buy.

Something in a recipe is wrong. What can I do?

Tap Report a problem (ভুল জানান) in the recipe and tell us what's wrong, for example a wrong amount or ingredient name. Please don't include personal details.

Allergen hints in the app are guidance, not medical advice. Check product labels and food safety yourself.
