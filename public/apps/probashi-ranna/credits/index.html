Credits · কৃতজ্ঞতা

Thank you to everyone whose work goes into Probashi Ranna.

Recipe texts and photos

Where a recipe text, translation or photo needs a credit, the app shows it in the recipe itself, together with the licence where one applies.

Bengali typography, fonts and open-source components

The fonts used to display Bengali and the open-source components the apps are built with are credited in the apps.

Rights questions

If you hold rights to a text or photo in the app and would like it credited differently, corrected or removed, write to hello@iearul.xyz with the recipe name and what you'd like us to change. In the app you can also use Report a problem (ভুল জানান) in the recipe and choose Rights or copyright. We check every request.
