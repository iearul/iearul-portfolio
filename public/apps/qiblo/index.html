Questions

When can I download Qiblo?

Qiblo launches on Google Play and the App Store before Ramadan 2027. A test version is planned for the end of November 2026: sign up as a beta tester and you get an invite from Google Play or TestFlight.

Does Qiblo know where I am?

Only on your phone. The app uses your location to calculate prayer times, the Qibla direction and the distance to mosques, and never sends it to us. You can also pick a city instead.

How does the halal scanner decide?

Each product is checked from its ingredients, with the reasons shown for alcohol and animal ingredients. Recipes change, so check the label if in doubt. Missing product or wrong result? Report it right in the app.

Which calculation method does Qiblo use?

The one of your community. You choose it in the app, for example DITIB, an Arab or a South Asian mosque.
