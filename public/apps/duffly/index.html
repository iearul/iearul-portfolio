Your trip. Your packing list.

Duffly is a packing list for your trips. Build a list and see what still needs attention before you leave – no sign-up, no ads, and your lists stay on your device.

What Duffly does

A useful list in a few steps

Choose dates, trip type and activities. Every suggestion says why it appears, such as “7 days + 1 spare”. Untick what you don't need before the list is created.

Bought and packed, kept separate

What you still need to buy and what is already in the bag stay visible as separate states. A “To buy” list collects open purchases.

Ready to leave

Missing essentials, open purchases, your tasks before you leave and weight notes at a glance.

Honest weights

When weights are missing you see a partial weight, not a misleading total. Bag limits are your own entries; the app doesn't check airline rules.

Private and offline

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your lists stay on your device and you export backups yourself. Uninstalling may remove local data.

Questions

Do I need an account?

No. The app works without signing in. Only buying and restoring Lifetime Pro needs your Google Play or Apple account and an internet connection.

Are my lists uploaded?

No. Your trips and lists are stored on your device and are not sent to us. Details in the privacy policy.

How do I move to a new phone?

Export a backup on the old phone and import it on the new one – also between Android and iPhone. Automatic device backups don't include your trips. Step by step on the help page.

Does Pro work on Android and iPhone?

No. Purchases are separate per platform. On a new device of the same platform, use “Restore purchase” to get Pro back.

Does the app check baggage rules?

No. Weights and bag limits are your own estimates. The app doesn't check airline rules, travel documents or entry requirements.

Suggestions are editable packing aids, not travel requirements. Check your travel documents and airline rules separately.
