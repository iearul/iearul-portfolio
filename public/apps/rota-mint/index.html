Questions

Is this my payslip?

No. RotaMint estimates gross pay from what you enter. It is not a payslip and not tax, payroll or legal advice. Your contract, collective agreement or the law may differ.

Does RotaMint know legal premiums or public holidays?

No, on purpose. Premiums are your own rules, and you mark holidays yourself because they differ by region.

What happens when the clocks change?

RotaMint counts the time actually worked: a 22:00–06:00 night shift is seven hours in March and nine in October.

Where is my data?

On your device. If you use Google or Apple device backups, they may include it. Details in the privacy policy.

Amounts are estimates based on your settings, not an official payslip.
