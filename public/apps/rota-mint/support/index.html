Questions

How do I enter a night shift?

Pick “Night” or turn on “Ends next day”. The shift stays one entry; monthly totals split it at midnight and at the end of the month.

Why does it say “Earnings not set”?

The shift has no hourly rate. Set one in the shift or in Settings. RotaMint does not show a misleading €0 instead.

I changed my hourly rate and past months stayed the same.

That is intended: each shift keeps the rate it was saved with. Use “Use current rate” in the editor to update a single shift.

How do I restore my Pro purchase?

Sign in on the device with the same Google Play or Apple account and tap Settings › Restore purchase in the app. A purchase on Android does not carry over to iPhone, and vice versa.

Refunds

Purchases go through the stores. Request a refund from Google Play or via Apple. After a refund, Pro is removed at the next check.

How do I delete my data?

Settings › Delete all data in the app. We do not store any app data about you.

New phone

The data lives only on your device. It moves to a new phone through Google's or Apple's device backup or transfer if you use it. This version has no export.
